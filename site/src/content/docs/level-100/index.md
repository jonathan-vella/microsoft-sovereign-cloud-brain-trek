Level 100 explains what Microsoft Sovereign Cloud is and which products deliver it. You learn the vocabulary of
digital sovereignty, the three deployment models, and the products that make up the Sovereign Private Cloud. Each
module ends with a knowledge check.

The level is written for architects, presales engineers, and account teams who need to explain these options to a
customer. It stays at the "what and why" depth. Level 200 covers design and planning, and Level 300 covers
production architecture.

## How to work through the level

Modules 1 and 2 give you the concepts that the product modules build on, so take them first. You can take
modules 3 to 6 in any order, but module 3 helps with modules 5 and 6 because both run on Azure Local.

:::note[Preview products]
Foundry Local on Azure Local and Agentic Retrieval (formerly Edge RAG) are in preview
([Foundry Local on Azure Local](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/azure-sovereign-clouds/private/foundry-local/overview),
[Agentic Retrieval](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/azure-arc/agents-tools-foundry-local/whats-new)). Module 5
marks preview features where they appear.
:::

## Before you start

You need a basic understanding of cloud service and deployment models, security and compliance terms, and Azure
regions and management tools. [Level 50](/level-50/) covers all three.

## After Level 100

Go on to [Level 200](/level-200/) for design and planning: deeper Azure Local and Azure Arc topics, Foundry Local
deployment, presales sizing, compliance mapping, Sovereign Public Cloud controls, and the Sovereign Private Cloud
stack.
