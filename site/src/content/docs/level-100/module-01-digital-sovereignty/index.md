Digital sovereignty is the starting point for the rest of Level 100. Before you compare cloud models, Azure Local, Azure Arc, or Foundry Local, you need a clear vocabulary for what must stay local, who can operate the environment, and which controls prove the design meets policy.

The module keeps each idea on its own page: what sovereignty means, where data lives, Microsoft's European commitments and the EU Data Boundary, who can operate and access the environment, and the EU regulations customers bring up most. Product detail comes in later modules.
